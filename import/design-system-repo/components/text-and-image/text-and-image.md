# Text and Image

## Short description
Split section pairing one image with an eyebrow, headline, short paragraph, and a single primary CTA. Three image treatments: Fixed Image, Cutout, Two Column.

## Content
- Eyebrow: short uppercase kicker, one to three words.
- Headline: one sentence, one to two lines.
- Body: one paragraph, roughly 40 to 60 words.
- CTA: one primary button; verb-first label.
- Image: Fixed Image and Two Column take rectangular photos (auto-cropped); Cutout takes a transparent PNG carrying its own shape.

## Marketing content notes
- This is the page opener in most stacks; lead with the value statement, not the org name.
- One CTA only. If two actions compete, this is the wrong component.
- Cutout suits portrait/people photography; Two Column suits landscape and environmental shots.

## Content system
Follows the library's Feature Storytelling technique (see
content-system.md): one feature-benefit bridge, told the same
way across all three image treatments (fixed-image, cutout,
two-column)—the story doesn't change, only the imagery format.
