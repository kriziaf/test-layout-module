# Heading Block

## Short description
Reusable section header: eyebrow, title, supporting copy, and an optional secondary (outline) CTA. Three layouts (Center, Left Justified, Two Column), each with a no-CTA version.

## Content
- Eyebrow: category or campaign kicker, uppercase.
- Title: headline-small scale; one line preferred, two max.
- Body: one to two sentences of framing copy.
- CTA (optional): secondary button; use when the section itself has a destination.
- Two Column places eyebrow and title left, body and CTA right.

## Marketing content notes
- This block also serves as the embedded header inside Feature List, Horizontal Cards, and the Two Column Form — copy written here sets the tone for the section below it.
- Skip the CTA when the section's cards carry their own links; competing CTAs dilute both.
