# Form

## Short description
Lead-capture form with five inputs, a message textarea, and a secondary (outline) submit. Two layouts: Two Column (heading and intro left, fields right) and Stacked (centered single column).

## Content
- Two Column: left column carries the heading block (eyebrow, headline small), the required-fields note, and one intro paragraph with an optional inline link.
- Stacked: centered header, then fields.
- Fields: labels above inputs; optional fields marked with "(optional)" next to the label. Textarea includes helper text left and a character count right.
- Submit label defaults to a single word.

## Marketing content notes
- Keep the required-note verbatim: "All fields are required unless marked optional" — it is the pattern users learn.
- Intro copy should say what happens after submitting (response time, who follows up).
- Helper text under the textarea is guidance, not legal copy; keep it under ten words.

## Content system
Follows the library's Conversion technique (see
content-system.md): value-exchange framing, friction-reduction
language. Both variants share the "Join us today" signup
content—matching the Homepage template’s form—reshaped per
