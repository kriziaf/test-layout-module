# Icon Cards

## Short description
Outlined tap-target cards, each a centered icon over a short heading. Two counts: 3-Up (one row) and 6-Up (three by two).

## Content
- Card heading: two to four words, sentence case.
- Icon: one Phosphor icon per card, swapped per destination.
- Every card is a link; there is no body copy by design.

## Marketing content notes
- These are wayfinding, not persuasion: headings should name destinations or tasks ("Find care", "Refill a prescription").
- Do not mix task cards and promo cards in one grid.
- 3-Up for primary tasks, 6-Up for directory-style hubs.
