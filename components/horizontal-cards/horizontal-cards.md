# Horizontal Cards

## Short description
Side-by-side content cards under a left-justified heading block. Three types: Icon (compact task rows), Image Small (140px photo), Image Large (one wide card per row with a 375px photo).

## Content
- Icon: Phosphor icon, two-line title, link. No body.
- Image Small: photo, title, short body (about 20 words), link.
- Image Large: photo, uppercase subtitle label, title, body, optional link.
- Whole card is tappable in Icon and Image Small; Image Large uses its inline link.

## Marketing content notes
- Image Small bodies get truncation pressure at tablet widths; front-load the value.
- Image Large is an editorial/feature spotlight — one or two per page, not a feed.
- Subtitle in Image Large is a category label, not a second headline.
